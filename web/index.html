Is the move worth it?

Moving a route to a new factory has a one-time cost (setting up production, moving stock). A move is recommended only if its yearly saving pays that back in time. The dataset has no switching cost, so these are business assumptions: change them to test the decision.
Yearly saving = estimated saving per order × 2025 orders on the route. Payback = switching cost ÷ yearly saving.
